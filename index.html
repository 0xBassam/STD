Compliance Frameworks We Support

Navigating Saudi Arabia's cybersecurity regulatory landscape with precision and confidence.

NCA

NCA ECC

Essential Cybersecurity Controls

The mandatory cybersecurity baseline issued by the National Cybersecurity Authority for all Saudi government agencies and critical national infrastructure operators.

SAMA

SAMA CSF

Cybersecurity Framework

Cybersecurity requirements mandated by the Saudi Arabian Monetary Authority for all banks, insurance companies, and financial sector entities operating in the Kingdom.

PDPL

PDPL

Personal Data Protection Law

Saudi Arabia's comprehensive data privacy regulation governing the collection, processing, storage, and transfer of personal data for all entities operating in the Kingdom.
